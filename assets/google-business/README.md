## Real-photo replacement brief

Use original photos of actual work or the actual operation. Do not upload the website's illustrative silver coupe or generated film as proof of work.

1. **Cover:** a finished vehicle in soft daylight, clean three-quarter composition, vehicle dominant, uncluttered background. Include the actual branded rig only if its identity is legible and accurate. Take a horizontal original with extra room for Google's crops.
2. **Finish:** a close-up showing a clean, reflective panel with believable reflections.
3. **Interior:** a clean seat/dashboard photo with even exposure, personal items removed.
4. **Work:** a real service in progress, with the tool touching the intended surface.
5. **Operation:** a clear, current shot of the actual equipment/rig or team. Only upload with the appropriate consent.

Upload accurate imagery of this business with rights to publish it. Avoid screenshots, duplicate near-identical pictures, stock work, customer addresses, visible private belongings, and captions that invent results.

Google's published guidance: JPG or PNG, 10 KB–5 MB, recommended 720 × 720 px (minimum 250 × 250); well lit, in focus, and reality represented without significant alteration or excessive AI. A selected cover is not guaranteed to be the first image shown. Source: https://support.google.com/business/answer/6123536?hl=en and https://support.google.com/business/answer/6103862?hl=en.
